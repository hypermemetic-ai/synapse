=========== 3. WHAT THIS DOES NOT CLAIM ===========
RFC 002 6.8 makes cancellation cooperative: a conformant Cancelled asserts that the
signal was DELIVERED to the turn and that the turn RESOLVED with Cancelled.
Unsubscribing tears down the subscription -- proven above -- but it does not reach
plexus_core::runtime::LiveTurns::cancel_turn, which is reachable from NO wire method
and takes a TurnId the server advertises nowhere except inside a callback's request_id.
So no Cancelled terminal comes back, and synapse does not pretend one did: it renders
its OWN terminal, coded synapse:client_cancelled, whose message says the server was
not observed to confirm. Closing that is a server-supply change; it is filed as the
PLX-84 10.2 amendment (dated 2026-07-26) and NOT faked here.

Note also that the demo server's slow() handler does NOT poll HandlerContext::cancelled().
Making it do so would prove nothing about the client and would hide exactly this gap.
